YOUR VOYAGE — REFLECTION QUESTIONS
QUESTION 1 OF 10

Which character did you start with? Describe where they are from and a few facts about the society they lived in — including major religious beliefs and the political system.

QUESTION 2 OF 10

Which trade goods did you buy in your home port? Describe why at least one of these items was cheapest at your starting port.

Think about: climate, skilled labor, low wages, geographic proximity to other cultures, or access to raw materials.

QUESTION 3 OF 10

What dangers did you face on your ocean voyages? Choose one danger and explain what it reveals about the limits of medieval maritime technology or governance.

QUESTION 4 OF 10

What problems did you face when trying to trade in a foreign port? Why did this problem exist — what made foreign trade structurally more difficult than trading at home?

QUESTION 5 OF 10

What technologies or adaptations do you think could have made the journey or trading in a foreign country less dangerous and more reliable?

Possible ideas: magnetic compass, astrolabe, reliable maps, knowledge of monsoon wind routes, local contacts, knowledge of the local language, or information from other merchants about prices.

QUESTION 6 OF 10

Which trade goods did you buy in the foreign port? Describe why at least one of these items was cheapest in that port.

Think about: climate, skilled labor, geographic proximity to other cultures, or access to raw materials.

QUESTION 7 OF 10

Why would it take roughly a year to travel from one port to another and back again?

QUESTION 8 OF 10

Why do consumers want these goods? How would their lives benefit from attaining these high-priced items?

A "dinar" is worth roughly a day's wages for a laborer in the Middle Ages. Think about: luxury status, medicine, spices, textiles, and the social meaning of rare goods.

QUESTION 9 OF 10

Why do you think no empire attempted to take control over these trade routes in the way that land-based empires took control over land routes like the Silk Road?

QUESTION 10 OF 10

Which universal religions spread along these routes? Explain one specific way a religion might spread through merchant contact rather than military conquest.
